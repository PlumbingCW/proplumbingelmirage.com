Frozen Pipes and Burst Pipe Repair in El Mirage, AZ

A hard freeze is not a yearly event here, which is exactly why homes are not built or prepared for one.

By the El Mirage Pro Plumbing team · Published October 8, 2026 · Updated October 8, 2026

The short answer

Hard freezes are uncommon in El Mirage. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Youngtown station list a record low of 20F, and that is the record, not a typical winter night. Most years pass without a pipe freezing.

On the rare cold night, though, an exposed pipe can freeze and split. If a pipe freezes, open the tap, warm the pipe gently and shut off the main if you see water. If one bursts, shut off the main right away and call a licensed plumber.

Should El Mirage homeowners worry about frozen pipes at all?

A little, and mostly because nobody plans for it. The Valley does get the occasional freeze warning. One covered the Phoenix area in January 2023, and local plumbers at the time told people to disconnect hoses, keep water moving and wrap exposed pipes. That is sensible advice, and it fits a rare event.

Do not expect your house to behave like a northern home with buried or heated lines. Pipes that sit outside, in an unheated garage or in an exposed wall are the ones at risk. How your own house is plumbed is something to ask a plumber, since we cannot say what is typical for a specific neighborhood.

Pipes froze and nothing is leaking yet: what now?

Slow, gentle action matters here. Fast heat on a frozen pipe can do more harm than the ice.

  1. Find the frozen spot

    A tap with no flow, or only a trickle, usually means a freeze somewhere upstream. Check exposed pipes by feel and by sight for frost or bulges.

  2. Open the tap

    Leave the affected faucet open. Melting ice needs a path out, and moving water helps.

  3. Warm the pipe slowly

    Use a hair dryer on a low setting, warm towels or a heating pad. Never use an open flame or a torch.

  4. Watch for leaks

    As ice thaws, a crack can start to leak. Know where the main shutoff is, and check the pipe as it warms.

  5. Call if you cannot reach it

    If the frozen section is in a wall or under a slab, call a plumber instead of tearing things open.

A pipe has burst: do this first

Shut off the water at the main valve, then open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line. Turn off power to any area with standing water if you can reach the breaker safely, and keep people out of wet rooms.

Call the 24/7 line. A plumber can talk you through the next steps and plan a burst pipe repair once the water is off. Take photos of the damage before you clean up, since your insurer will ask.

Preparing for a rare cold night

Fifteen minutes before a forecast freeze is enough for most homes.

  • Unhook garden hoses and cover outdoor faucets.
  • Wrap or cover any exposed pipe, including irrigation and pool equipment.
  • Open cabinet doors on exterior walls so heated room air can reach the pipes.
  • Let a faucet drip slightly overnight on the coldest runs.
  • Keep the garage door closed and the heat on if you are away.
  • Find the main shutoff and make sure everyone at home knows how to turn it.
  • Save the plumber's number where you can find it in the dark.

Thawed pipe or burst pipe: what repair looks like

The fix depends on whether the pipe cracked while it was frozen. Often you will not know until it warms.

Frozen but intact

  • Water flows normally once thawed.
  • No stains, drips or sound of running water.
  • A plumber may check fittings and insulate the spot.

Cracked or split

  • Water sprays, pools or stains a wall or ceiling.
  • The damaged section has to be cut out and replaced.
  • Hidden leaks may need leak detection to find them.

What shapes the price of a burst pipe repair?

The pipe is usually the smallest part of the bill. Cost follows where the break is, how much wall or ceiling must be opened, what material the pipe is and how much water damage came with it. A split in an open garage line is a different job than one inside a finished wall.

Water damage cleanup and drywall repair are often separate from the plumbing work and may involve other trades. Ask for a clear price for the plumbing before work starts, and ask what is not included. Your insurer may want to see photos and the plumber's invoice.

Is the pipe leak outside, between the street and the house?

A wet patch in the yard or a drop in pressure with nothing wrong inside points toward the line running to the house. That is a water line repair question, not a frozen pipe one, and cold weather is rarely the cause in this area.

Before anyone digs, Arizona law requires a call to Arizona 811. It is free, and utilities have two full working days to mark their lines. Hand tools only within 24 inches of marks.

El Mirage in a freeze: what the record actually shows

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Youngtown station list an annual average of 8.34 inches of precipitation, a record high of 122F and a record low of 20F. A record low of 20F means a hard freeze can happen but is unusual. Plan for the rare night with cheap steps like covers and open cabinet doors, and skip anything built for a northern winter.

The same advice holds for nearby towns, so a neighbor in Youngtown or Peoria can use it too, though each town's own utility and permit rules differ. Monsoon season runs June 15 to September 30, and those storms bring flooding risk, not freezing, so keep the two risks separate.
