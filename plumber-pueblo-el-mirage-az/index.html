Plumbing in Pueblo El Mirage

Pueblo El Mirage is a gated golf and RV resort at 11201 N El Mirage Road, and this page is for residents and owners there. Call (623) 301-9042, describe the leak, clog or water heater problem and ask whether a plumber can help at your lot.

If water is running, close the shutoff for your home first. Then contact park management, because access through the gate and ownership of the lines often decide what happens next.

What Pueblo El Mirage is

Good Sam describes Pueblo El Mirage RV and Golf Resort as a 55+ gated community with 425 spaces, full hookups, an 18-hole golf course, a pool and a restaurant, open all year. The address is 11201 N El Mirage Road.

City records agree it is a manufactured-home setting. A 2021 staff report on a final plat at that address covers 4.85 acres zoned for a mobile park, and it lists the neighboring land on all four sides as high density manufactured homes.

The resort is private and gated. We have no information about how it is managed or who owns the water, sewer and gas lines inside it, so we do not claim to know.

Who owns the line? Ask before you repair

In many manufactured-home communities, the park owns the main lines and the homeowner owns everything from a connection point to the house. We cannot say where that point sits at Pueblo El Mirage. It can differ by lot and by park rules.

Before paying for a repair, ask park management which part of the line is yours and whether outside plumbers may enter. A repair on the wrong side of the connection can be refused or can become a billing argument later.

A good call to us includes your lot number, the type of home and a clear description of where the water is. We can then say whether a plumber should look at the fixture, the heater or the line.

Homeowner jobs and plumber jobs

Small tasks are fine to do yourself. Anything involving a line, gas or a heater that is leaking belongs to a plumber.

Usually fine for you

  • Close the shutoff for your home when water is running.
  • Replace a showerhead or a faucet aerator.
  • Photograph a stain or leak for park management.

Better left to a plumber

  • A tank that leaks from its body.
  • Any gas smell or pilot problem.
  • Water coming up from the ground near the home.
  • A repair on the line between pedestal and home.

Seasonal stays and shutting a home down

Many 55+ resorts have residents who leave for part of the year. When a home is unoccupied, a leaking hose or heater can run unnoticed. Close the home shutoff when you leave and tell a neighbor or park staff where it is.

Compact water heaters in manufactured homes can be in a closet or a service compartment, and the label on the tank gives its age. A tank over ten years old is worth a check before a long absence. This is general guidance, since we have not seen individual homes here.

El Mirage reports hardness of 4 to 9 grains per gallon in its 2024 report. The resort may use its own water supply, so ask management whether that figure applies to you.

Before a plumber comes to a Pueblo El Mirage lot

Gates and park rules add steps, so plan for them.

Tell the gate

Contact park management so the plumber can enter, and give them your lot number.

Know the shutoff

Find the valve for your home and the pedestal connection. Close the home shutoff if water is running.

Ask who owns the line

Management can usually say which side of the connection is yours.

Note the equipment

Write down the water heater brand, age and fuel type, plus any model number you can read.

Plumbing pages that fit manufactured homes

These are the most likely matches for the problems residents describe.

  • A heater that has quit may be covered under water heater repair.
  • A body leak usually means water heater replacement.
  • Wet ground or a moving meter points to leak detection.
  • A dripping tap is handled by faucet repair.
