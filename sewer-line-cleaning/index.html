Sewer Line Cleaning in El Mirage, AZ

Sewer line cleaning in El Mirage, AZ means clearing the main drain pipe that carries all of the home's wastewater out toward the street connection. When that pipe blocks, water has nowhere to go and comes up at the lowest opening, usually a tub, shower, or floor drain. Our 24/7 line is answered at any hour. Stop using water while you wait.

The main line is bigger and longer than the pipes under your sinks, and it runs underground. That changes the tools, the access, and the questions to ask. This page covers what a backup looks like, how plumbers clear a main line, and what it means when the blockage keeps returning.

What to do when the sewer line backs up

A main line backup is a health problem as well as a plumbing one, because sewage carries bacteria. Treat it as an emergency even if only a little water has come up.

Every flush, shower, and load of laundry sends more water toward the same blockage. The fastest way to limit damage is to stop adding to it.

  • Stop all water use, including toilets, showers, washers, and dishwashers.
  • Keep children and pets out of the room and away from the drain.
  • Do not walk through standing sewage in bare feet, and keep it off bedding and furniture.
  • Skip chemical drain products. They do not clear a main line and add hazard.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe which drains are backing up.

Sewage coming up in the tub, shower, or floor drain

Wastewater from a house flows by gravity through the main line to a connection outside the property. When a blockage forms downstream of every branch, water backs up to the lowest fixture first. That is why a tub or floor drain fills while a sink upstairs still seems fine.

A second sign is a toilet that bubbles or gurgles when you run a washing machine. The water displaced by the drain pushes air back through the nearest open trap. A third is several drains slowing at once, which a single sink clog does not explain.

These patterns separate a main line problem from a fixture clog. If only one sink is slow, our drain cleaning page covers that case instead. If the symptoms match the ones above, the blockage is likely in the main line and needs a plumber with the right access.

What the plumber does on a sewer cleaning call

The details vary by house, but a main line call tends to follow this order.

  1. Find the cleanout

    A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives direct access to the main line, often outside near the house. Finding it saves time and avoids pulling a toilet.

  2. Confirm it is the main line

    The plumber tests fixtures and watches where water backs up, so the cable goes into the right pipe.

  3. Cable the line

    A larger cable than a sink machine runs toward the blockage and breaks it apart or pulls it out.

  4. Read what comes back

    Roots, grease, wipes, and sludge each have a recognizable look and point to different causes.

  5. Flush and test

    Water is run through the line and fixtures to check that everything drains freely.

  6. Recommend next steps

    If the blockage was roots or something that returns, the plumber may suggest hydro jetting or a camera look. You get a clear price before work starts.

Main sewer cleaning versus a branch clog

The wording on a service call can blur the difference. These lists show how the two jobs differ.

Main sewer line cleaning

  • Affects several fixtures or the lowest drain.
  • Usually cleared from a cleanout outside the house.
  • Larger cable or jetting equipment.
  • Often leads to a camera look if it returns.

Branch or fixture clog

  • Affects one sink, tub, or toilet.
  • Cleared through the fixture or a nearby access point.
  • Smaller cable or hand tools.
  • Often prevented by changing habits.

Sewer drain cleaning, main line cleaning, and what the words mean

People search for the same job under several names. Main sewer cleaning, sewer drain cleaning, and clearing the main line all describe opening the large pipe that leaves the house. Ask the plumber exactly which pipe they will clear and from where, so the price covers the job you need.

None of these terms mean the work is done at the city's public main in the street. That pipe belongs to the city. If you suspect the blockage is out there, ask El Mirage customer service or Public Works, because a plumber cannot service the public main for you.

City sewer or septic: confirm before you assume

Some properties are on city sewer and some are on septic. If you are not sure whether yours is on sewer or septic, ask the city before you assume. A septic system needs a different kind of service than a main line.

Why a blocked main line comes back

One clearing does not always end the problem. These are the usual reasons a main line blocks again.

  • Wipes and hygiene products that do not break down, even when labeled flushable.
  • Grease from the kitchen that cools and builds on the pipe wall.
  • Roots that enter through a joint or crack and grow back after cutting.
  • A sag in the pipe where water and solids settle.
  • A pipe damaged or shifted by ground movement or past construction.
  • Scale buildup. The city's 2024 report lists hardness from 4 to 9 grains per gallon, average 7, and hard water can add to scale over the years.
