Local notes

What plumbing looks like in El Mirage homes

El Mirage grew fast between 2000 and 2010, and Census-based data puts the median home build year around 2004. Homes that age are past their first set of water heaters and rubber supply lines. If yours is more than ten years old, ask a plumber to check the tank, the shutoff valves and the hoses under every sink.

The city pumps its water from nine wells. The 2024 city water quality report lists hardness between 4 and 9 grains per gallon, with an average of 7. That is hard water, and mineral scale builds on heating elements, faucet aerators and valve seats. Water heater repair and faucet repair calls often trace back to scale that built up slowly.

Summer storms bring the monsoon from June 15 through September 30. Heavy rain can send water toward yards and slabs, so a wet patch near the foundation is worth checking before you assume it is rain. A leak detection visit separates runoff from a leak in the line.

Grand Avenue, which is US 60, runs through the middle of town and the Agua Fria River borders the east side. If your home or shop sits off Grand Avenue near the river, the same 24/7 line covers you. Before anyone digs for a buried line, Arizona law requires a call to Arizona 811.
