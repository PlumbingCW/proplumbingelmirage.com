Plumbing in Sundial

Sundial is the name real estate records use for a group of subdivisions in El Mirage, Arizona, and this page is for homeowners there. Call (623) 301-9042, describe the leak, clog or water heater problem and ask about plumbing service for your address.

If water is running where it should not be, close the main valve first. If the house has been empty for days and you find water, shut off the main before you do anything else and photograph what you see.

What Sundial means in El Mirage

Listing directories show Sundial as four separate subdivisions: Sundial 1, Sundial 2, Sundial 3 and Sundial 4. One of them, Sundial 3, is described as a single-family community built between 2000 and 2006. Homes in that listing sit on streets such as W Cherry Hills Drive, W Laurel Lane and W Shaw Butte Drive.

Trulia also shows a Sundial IV Neighborhood Park on its Sundial page. The City of El Mirage pages we read do not name the subdivisions, so we treat Sundial as a name residents and agents use, not an official boundary.

Sundial sits near other 2000s subdivisions, including Parque Verde and Rancho El Mirage, which share a ZIP code and some of the same streets in listing data. We do not give those their own pages because the lines between them are not published.

A home that sits empty part of the year

NeighborhoodScout publishes census tract figures under the name Sundial. In that tract, 27.3 percent of housing is seasonally occupied and the vacancy rate is 29.8 percent. A tract can cover more than the Sundial subdivisions, so treat those numbers as a hint about the area, not a count of your street.

It still points to a practical risk. When no one is home, a leak keeps running until someone notices. A failed hose or a split tank can do real damage in a few days.

Before leaving a home for weeks, close the main water valve and ask a neighbor or property manager to check the house. If you would rather keep water on for irrigation, ask a plumber about shutting off the indoor supply only.

Leaving the house for the summer

Seasonal residents and anyone traveling can follow this order. None of it needs special tools.

  1. Find and test the main valve

    Turn it closed and open it again once while you are home. A valve that will not move is better found now than in the middle of a leak.

  2. Set the water heater

    Read the label on your tank for a vacation or low setting. Do not guess with gas controls.

  3. Check the visible hoses

    Look under sinks and behind the toilet and washer for drips, bulges or crusty mineral rings.

  4. Leave a contact

    Give a neighbor your number and the location of the main valve so they can shut it in a hurry.

Hard water and monsoon season

El Mirage reports hardness between 4 and 9 grains per gallon, averaging 7, in its 2024 water quality report. Over twenty years, scale on a water heater and faucet parts adds up.

Monsoon season runs from June 15 to September 30. Heavy rain can send water toward yards and low drains, so keep cleanouts and drain covers clear before the season starts.
