Privacy Policy

Short and plain. Last updated October 23, 2026.

What this site collects

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This data helps keep the site running and secure.

Information you share by phone

When you call, you may share your name, address and a description of the problem. We use these details to arrange plumbing service, to give you a price and to follow up on your job.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and third parties

This site embeds Google Maps in the coverage area section. Google may set cookies when the map loads under its own privacy policy. Phone calls are handled by a telephone provider that may keep call records.

Your choices and contact

To ask about information you shared by phone, call (623) 301-9042 or email contact@proplumbingelmirage.com. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
